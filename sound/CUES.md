# Mars Life Line — gentle sound palette

Simple, quiet, friendly and grounded. No scary drones, squeaks, distorted robot
voices, piercing alarms or sudden loud effects. Pebble is a small tracked helper.
All files are original procedural synthesis; no downloaded recordings or music.

- **Footstep regolith (4 variants):** A padded boot compresses fine dust, followed
  by a short grit settling. Close through a helmet, low and soft, heard often.
- **Footstep cabin (4 variants):** A rubber sole presses a damped metal floor.
  Contact, quiet panel resonance, then release. Short and close, never a clang.
- **Pebble drive:** A little electric motor and rubber tracks over dust. Smooth
  low motor body with restrained rolling texture, looping only while moving nearby.
- **Pebble turn:** A short gentle motor adjustment; soft start, brief roll, stop.
- **Pebble hello / happy / think / worried / reboot / charge:** A warm electronic
  voice using a few consonant notes with overlapping tails and changing harmonics.
  Friendly acknowledgement, success, thought, low battery, restart and docking.
  Worried is an occasional subdued descending cue, never a siren.
- **Pebble clean / grip:** Soft rotating brush on a panel; small damped gripper
  mechanism with contact and a quiet latch. Close only when the robot is nearby.
- **UI click / open / close / confirm / notice:** A soft electronic touch and
  warm small glass-like notes. Fast, restrained and distinct by function; no hover sound.
- **Door:** A padded latch releases, a short low servo slides, then a soft stop.
- **Airlock:** Smooth filtered air builds and releases, low and non-threatening;
  repeats during the actual pressure cycle and stops immediately on completion.
- **Tool / switch / harvest / message / complete:** Small metal tool contact,
  a damped switch, soft leaf rustle, friendly received signal, a warm success motif.
  Heard only on relevant actions; none are loud or urgent.
- **Landing entry / thrust / touchdown:** Distant, low filtered turbulence,
  smooth engine body and a cushioned heavy settling impact. Comfortable headroom.
- **Hab ambience / suit ambience:** Very quiet ventilation and life support,
  gently breathing in level, seamless loops, no literal wind in the vacuum.
- **Space music — Pale Blue Morning, revised:** A 48-second floating ambient
  loop with no beat, knocks, taps, plucks, bells or other struck notes. Wide warm
  synth clouds rise over several seconds, slowly overlapping open D/G/A voicings.
  A very quiet airy layer and distant high sustained glows add a light space
  feeling. Smooth long reverb, gentle stereo movement, no ominous bass or dramatic
  build. Circular tails keep the loop continuous.

## Review

Pass 1 establishes the layered sources. Pass 2 softens upper-mid energy on
frequent effects, lowers mechanical texture and leaves more space in the music.
Both passes are retained under sound/renders for matched audition. Measurements
verify peaks, edges and spectra; they do not establish subjective listening quality.
The user rejected the first music: "knocking sound" and "not space at all".
The revision removes all contact/resonator notes and uses only slow synth clouds.
Audition at /audio/index.html. The revised direction awaits the user's ears.

## Ruled out for music

No knocking, mallets, felt-glass strikes, plucks, rhythmic taps or fast note attacks.
Space music should feel light, spacious and floating, rather than percussive.
