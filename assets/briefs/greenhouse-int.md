# greenhouse-int

Sole visual reference: `models and assets images/greenhouse-int.webp`. Inspected before authoring; kept unchanged.

Greenhouse interior shell cutaway scene (6m diameter, 4m high) with full foundation skirt, circular interior deck with subtle seam rings, and a prominent front entrance portal with steps and a cyan indicator light. The front half of the dome is open for cutaway viewing and gameplay access, while the rear half features the ivory triangular geodesic strut framework with octagonal slate hub connection plates and the warm tinted see-through glass shell. Inside, three grow tray stations are arranged around the perimeter (center rear, left, right), each equipped with trough bodies, front orange plates, soil beds, stylized plant foliage clusters, overhead canopy hoods, and downward-facing glowing magenta LED strips.

- `door`: Front entrance portal.
- `info_anchor`: Floating status tag anchor above the apex.
- `tray_1`, `tray_2`, `tray_3`: Grow tray station anchors.
