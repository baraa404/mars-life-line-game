# greenhouse-dome

Sole visual reference: `models and assets images/greenhouse-dome.webp`. Inspected before authoring; kept unchanged.

Geodesic dome exterior (6m diameter, 4m high) with segmented skirt foundation featuring alternating ivory structure panels, vibrant Mars orange vertical accent bands, and slate vertical corner buttress pillars. Chamfered transition collar leads to an ivory triangular geodesic strut framework with octagonal slate hub connection plates and an apex hub cap. Transparent warm glass dome shell reveals the interior containing a raised floor slab and three grow trays with dark soil beds, stylized plant foliage clusters, overhead canopy hoods, and downward-facing magenta LED grow lights. Front entrance portal features a heavy slate archway with 45-degree chamfered top shoulders, a horizontal cyan indicator light above the lintel, an orange inner jamb frame, clear sliding glass door, and three ivory access steps descending to ground level (Y = 0) flanked by slate cheek curbs.

Semantic nodes:
- `roof`: Geodesic glass dome and strut framework (for Station View see-through fade).
- `info_anchor`: Floating status tag anchor above the apex.
- `tray_1`, `tray_2`, `tray_3`: Interior grow tray anchors.

Generated Mars header materials; metres, +Y up, +X forward.
