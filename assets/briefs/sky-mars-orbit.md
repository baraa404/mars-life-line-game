# sky-mars-orbit

Sole visual reference: `models and assets images/sky-mars-orbit.webp`. Inspected before authoring; kept unchanged.

Stylized low-poly planet Mars sphere for orbital view and touchdown descent sequence with terracotta regolith-mid core sphere, winding equatorial Valles Marineris canyon rift in shadow plum, scattered impact craters with raised regolith-light rims and plum shadow floors, and an outer translucent pale peach atmospheric limb glow shell.

Generated Mars header materials; metres, +Y up, +X forward (features facing forward +X hemisphere).
