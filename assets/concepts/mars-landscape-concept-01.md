## Master scene prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: art-direction approval image for the playable Mars Life Line terrain, BEFORE any 3D modeling. Reference 1 is the approved game-world hero scene and must govern the visual style, palette and atmosphere. Reference 2 is the approved terrain asset sheet and governs rock silhouettes and layered mesas. Reference 3 is the terrain category crop. Create one beautiful, rich, coherent environment-only landscape concept, wide cinematic 16:9 frame, without typography or a layout border. Match the project's polished stylized low-poly 3D miniature game look: broad clean facets on geology, thick eroded landforms, soft warm directional sunlight and peach fill. Show a wide traversable dusty basin with a clear relatively flat central clearing and natural paths leading into the middle distance. Compose irregular half-buried terracotta rock clusters and flat fractured slabs in the foreground at both edges; varied pebbles, cobbles and angular boulders in natural uneven groups, sparse enough to read the dusty ground. Subtle wind-shaped sand ripples and softly mottled red-orange soil, shallow dry washes weaving through the basin, a readable shallow crater in the middle distance with a broken raised rim, rocky benches and talus at cliff bases. Dramatic large flat-topped mesas and stepped layered escarpments frame the background on both sides, with a lower saddle opening near the center and several distant ridgelines fading into pale peach atmospheric haze. Geological layering is clearly modeled with broad strata, sloping eroded buttresses, broken edges and ledges; avoid smooth conical mountains. Ground is smooth matte dusty soil, not a triangulated tiled surface. Rocks are irregular, squat, asymmetrical, with large planar faces and flat buried bases, never rounded repeated balls. Palette restricted to project terracotta #B95C40, warm ochre #E6A26A, muted plum shadows #765768, dusty rose sky #C98270 and pale peach horizon #F2BE92. Maintain natural warm color separation, readable shadow details and clean art direction. Richness comes from composition, terrain forms, scale variation and restrained detail, not photorealistic texture noise. Low first-person-adjacent viewpoint slightly elevated to reveal ground routes and crater. No characters, buildings, vehicles, vegetation, water, starfield, planet, UI, text or watermarks. This must feel like an inviting, explorable Mars game location and be practical to reconstruct with procedural terrain and modular faceted rock meshes.
