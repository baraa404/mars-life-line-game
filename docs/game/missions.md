# Mission implementation status

Touchdown is implemented. [Phase 3 verification](phase-3-verification.md) covers the shared gameplay foundation. Mission definitions and all branch details live in
[design Part 2 §4](../../game-design/02-missions-events-campaign.md).
Update the relevant section with setup, interactions, branches, proof and gaps
when each level is built. MVP levels are M0, M1 and M10.

## M0 — Touchdown (Phase 4, MVP)

Implemented: autonomous landing, suit check, ship and hab pressure cycles,
PEBBLE's route to the base, three startup choices and the first wake report.
See [Touchdown](phase-4-touchdown.md) for entry points, source credits and verification.

## M1 — Night Shift (Phase 5, MVP)

Planned: set the night-power plan, sleep, then inspect battery use and causes.

## M2 — Seeds (Phase 6)

Planned: choose and plant crops; connect light, water and power to growth.

## M3 — Ice Run (Phase 7)

Planned: rover trip, clean/dirty ice choice and water processing.

## M4 — Air You Can't See (Phase 8)

Planned: choose oxygen production and replace the scrubber cartridge.

## M5 — Dust Devil (Phase 9)

Planned: panel dust, manual cleaning, PEBBLE cleaning or waiting for a breeze.

## M6 — Power Delivery (Phase 10)

Planned: fission placement, towing and cable laying with side costs.

## M7 — Earth Is Listening… Slowly (Phase 11)

Planned: delayed advice, signal tracking and a local valve repair.

## M8 — Science or Safety (Phase 12)

Planned: EVA duration, sample collection and radiation trade-offs.

## M9 — Storm Season (Phase 13)

Planned: forecast dust opacity, load shedding and grit repairs.

## M10 — The Day the Sun Roared (Phase 14, MVP)

Planned: archived May 2024 solar event; choose the hab shelter, overhang or
finishing a repair; show dose and a real-data debrief. No event dataset or
mission script has been wired yet.

## M11 — Silence (Phase 15)

Planned: upload choices, conjunction blackout and Standing Orders.

## M12 — Harvest & Signal (Phase 16)

Planned: harvest, returning Earth signal, delayed outcomes and the ending.
